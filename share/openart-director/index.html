01OpenArt Director, where the whole film livesOpenArt Director,整条片子都住在这儿

You talk to it like a director and build the whole thing in one place. Start with the idea, then work through the story, characters, shots, voices, music, and revisions together.
Because it keeps the full project in context, you can make up to five minutes as one continuous film instead of generating a bunch of separate clips and stitching them back together. Same world, same face, same voice, all the way through. open it in another tab, then come back for the prompt

你像导演一样跟它说话,整件事在一个地方做完。从一个想法开始,然后故事、角色、分镜、配音、音乐、改稿,都和它一起过。
因为它一直记着整个项目,你可以一次做出五分钟连着不断的一整条片,而不是生成一堆分开的片段再拼回去。同一个世界、同一张脸、同一个声音,从头到尾。 另开一个标签页打开它,再回来拿 prompt

02the prompt that builds it with you陪你一起把它做出来的 prompt

Think of this less like a prompt, and more like giving your director a job.
Paste the whole thing as your first message. It’ll ask you one question at a time about your friend, what they love, what you want to say, and the little details you want to hide inside.
Then it turns your answers into the world, the story, and the shots.
No film language required. If you can describe your friend, you can make the card.
The only thing that stays is the spine: a light leads you through the world, each wish reveals itself in a different way, and the journey ends at the candle. Everything else is yours. paste it, then just answer ↓

别把它当成一段 prompt,当成你在给自己的导演派一件活。
整段复制,当第一条消息发过去。它会一次问你一个问题:你朋友是谁、她喜欢什么、你想说什么、还有你想藏在里面的那些小心思。
然后它把你的答案变成那个世界、那个故事和那些镜头。
不需要懂任何拍片的术语。你要是能把你朋友描述出来,你就能做出这张卡。
唯一不变的是那条脊梁:一点光带你穿过那个世界,每一句祝福各用各的方式显出来,这趟旅程最后落在蜡烛上。其余全都是你的。 贴进去,然后只管回答 ↓
