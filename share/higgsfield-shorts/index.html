A few things I learned

几条我学到的

01Choose the reference for the edit, not just the aesthetic.
Pay attention to the captions, pacing, transitions, and how the visuals support what someone is saying.
02Start with the cheaper generation mode.
That's what I chose for my first experiment. It's a practical way to test the workflow before committing to the higher-quality option.
03Treat the first output as a draft.
You can give Claude follow-up notes with timestamps to refine specific sections, depending on the editing options available.
04Check the final result.
Review the captions, visuals, scene order, B-roll, and ending before posting. If you're using a generated presenter, check visual and voice consistency too.
01选参考片看的是剪辑,不只是好看。
看它的字幕、节奏、转场,以及画面怎么配合说话的内容。
02先用便宜的生成模式。
我第一次试就是这么选的。先把流程跑通,再考虑高质量那个选项。
03把第一版当草稿。
可以给 Claude 带时间戳的修改意见,只改某一段,看它支持到什么程度。
04发之前检查成片。
字幕、画面、场景顺序、B-roll、结尾都过一遍。用了生成的主持人,再看一眼形象和声音是否一致。
