Privacy Policy

Lilian Content Stats

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Who this is

Lilian Content Stats is a private tool built and used by one person, Lilian Chiu, to look at her own social media post statistics in one place. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data it accesses

With the account owner's authorization, the app reads the following from her own TikTok account only:

  • Basic profile information (open id, display name)
  • Account statistics (follower count, video count)
  • A list of her own videos and their publicly visible metrics: view count, like count, comment count, share count, title, creation time and share link

The app does not access private messages, drafts, contacts, or any other person's account.

How the data is used

The data is displayed in a private dashboard that runs on the owner's own computer. It is used only to compare how her own posts perform across platforms.

How the data is stored

All retrieved data and access tokens are stored locally on the owner's personal computer, in a file readable only by her user account. Nothing is uploaded to a server, published, sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising, profiling or training.

How long it is kept

Data is kept only as long as the owner chooses. She can delete it at any time by deleting the local files.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke this app's access at any time in TikTok's settings, under Security and login, Manage app permissions. Revoking access immediately stops all further data retrieval.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone other than its single owner.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will replace this page.
